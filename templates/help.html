① This tool contains a set of questions. By answering honestly, you will gain insight into your mental wellbeing and receive a personalised classification.
② After clicking Proceed, you will start with the Basic Questionnaire — 11 everyday questions about sleep, lifestyle, and habits.
③ You will then take the GAD-7 Anxiety Score Test — 7 validated questions developed in 2006 to measure the severity of generalised anxiety.
④ Next comes the PHQ-9 Depression Score Test — 9 clinically validated questions developed in 1999 to measure depression severity.
⑤ Your Result Page will then show your mental health condition (Anxiety, Stress, Depression, or Normal) along with personalised solutions and a downloadable PDF report.
✓ All questions are compulsory. Choose the answer that best describes how you feel right now.
♡ Be honest — this is a safe, private space. Your honesty helps the system truly understand your emotional wellbeing.
⚠ Answers are immutable once submitted per section. Please fill each section thoughtfully before proceeding.
↓ After completing all sections, you can download your mental health report as a PDF for personal reference.
